Privacy Policy for MediBell: Pill Reminder & Tracker

Effective date: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MediBell mobile application collects, uses, stores and shares information. Basic medication reminders can be used without creating a MediBell account. Account-based Family Control features are optional and use secure cloud synchronization.

On first launch, MediBell asks the user to review and accept this Privacy Policy and then the application Terms of Use before opening the main screen. The documents can be viewed in English, Russian or Ukrainian in the app.

Information stored on your device

MediBell may store the following information locally:

  • people or family member names and relationship labels;
  • medication names, doses, instructions, schedules and reminder times;
  • medication stock, low-stock thresholds and intake history;
  • notification state, snooze state and app preferences;
  • language, theme, time format and timezone preferences.

Optional MediBell account and family data

If you sign in to Family Control, MediBell processes your email address, authentication user ID, profile name, family group membership, invitation status and permissions. Data you choose to synchronize may include family member labels, medications, dose instructions, schedules, timezone settings, stock information, medication events and history.

Family members see only data allowed by the group and permission model. Owners can invite members and manage permissions. Invited users must not share sign-in codes or invitation codes with unauthorized people.

Remote reminders and SOS

Family plans may process remote reminder requests and SOS events, including the sender name, sender account ID, family group, time, delivery state and individual acknowledgement state. SOS is sent only after the user presses and holds the SOS control. SOS is not connected to emergency services and is not a replacement for contacting local emergency services.

Subscriptions and abuse prevention

Google Play processes payment details. MediBell does not receive or store your card number. To verify access, MediBell processes the Google Play product ID, purchase state, expiry time and a one-way hash of the purchase token. When you are signed in, a verified family-plan entitlement is linked to your MediBell account so it can be restored after an update or reinstall.

A one-way device-derived identifier may be used to enforce the one-time 3-day trial and prevent repeated trial abuse. The raw device identifier is not stored by MediBell's backend.

How information is used

  • to schedule and display medication reminders;
  • to record Taken, Skipped and Snoozed actions;
  • to synchronize permitted family data and stock changes;
  • to deliver remote family reminders and SOS alerts;
  • to verify subscriptions, restore access and prevent abuse;
  • to protect account and family data from unauthorized access.

Service providers

MediBell uses Supabase for optional account authentication, database storage, access control, realtime synchronization and server functions. MediBell uses Google Play Billing and Google Play Developer APIs to sell and verify Android subscriptions. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy commitments. MediBell does not sell personal or health-related data and does not use it for advertising.

Security and retention

Network traffic is encrypted in transit. Cloud tables use access-control rules designed to limit data to authorized family members. Local data remains on the device until it is deleted, the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Cloud family data is retained while the account or family group is active and as reasonably needed for security, subscription verification, dispute handling and legal obligations.

Account and data deletion

You can sign out without deleting your account. To permanently delete a MediBell account and its associated cloud family data, use the Delete account action in MediBell account settings. You may also request deletion by emailing sudniksanya@gmail.com from the email address registered to the account. Subscription records required for fraud prevention or legal compliance may be retained in de-identified form. Google Play subscriptions must be cancelled separately in Google Play.

Children and family use

MediBell is not directed to children under 13. An adult family owner should manage invitations and permissions when medication reminders concern a child.

Your choices

You may use local reminders without joining a family account. You can revoke family permissions, leave a family, sign out, delete local data, request account deletion and manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play.

MediBell is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always follow instructions from a qualified healthcare professional. For an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as MediBell changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to Oleksandr Sudnyk at sudniksanya@gmail.com.
